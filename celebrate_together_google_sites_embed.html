01 / Meet the topic

Every celebration has a story!

Hi, friends! I am Tiki. 🎊 My friends celebrate in different ways. Some enjoy special food, some decorate their homes, and some meet their family and friends. What celebrations do you know?

In Malaysia, people celebrate Hari Raya Aidilfitri, Deepavali, Chinese New Year, Christmas, Gawai, Kaamatan and birthdays. We can learn about each other and show respect.

02 / Learning zone

Learn the celebration words

Look at each picture. Read the word and practise saying it aloud. Use the browser's text-to-speech button if available.

🎆

Fireworks

Bright lights in the sky.

Example: I can see fireworks.

🎁

Gift

Something you give to someone.

Example: This is a gift.

👘

Traditional clothes

Clothes worn for special occasions.

I wear traditional clothes.

🎂

Cake

A sweet food for a celebration.

This is a cake.

🏮

Lantern

A light used for decoration.

This is a lantern.

🍍

Food

Something people eat.

We share food.

🎵

Music

Sounds people listen to.

We listen to music.

🏠

Decoration

Something used to make a place look special.

The decoration is beautiful.

🎊

Celebration

A special time or event.

Gawai is a celebration.
